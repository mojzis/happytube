# Interview with Hoffman Coexistence Fellow Prospère Teberd

Prospère TEBERD has been a Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden Hoffman Coexistence Fellow since 2024. Graduating from the fellowship this year, Prospère’s work as a Mondika Gorilla Project Site Research Assistant for Congo-Apes in the Djéké Triangle of Congo has allowed him to act as a facilitator of human-wildlife coexistence in the field. His work centers around promoting the conservation of Western lowland gorillas through sustainable ecotourism and community engagement in northern Congo.
