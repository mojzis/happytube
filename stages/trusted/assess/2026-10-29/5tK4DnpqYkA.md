# World's Coolest Jobs | Explorer Academy Compilation | Nat Geo Kids

Watch this Explorer Academy: The Truth Behind... compilation on Nat Geo Kids YouTube!

Meet Nat Geo explorers with some of the coolest jobs on Earth (and beyond!):

00:00 M Jackson: Glacier Explorer
M Jackson studies ice sheets around the world to learn their secrets and undercover the truth about life near glaciers.

03:15 Erika Bergman: Finding Aliens Underwater
Explorer Erika Bergman dives into adventure in her submersible vehicle and pilots her way to discovery!

05:52 Zoltan Takacs: Venomous Animals
Zoltan Takacs seeks out life-saving medications from the world’s deadliest creatures.

09:47 Genevieve Von Petzinger: Ancient Cave Art
Genevieve Von Petzinger follows the trail of prehistoric art, from European caves to scorching deserts of California.

13:38 Branwen William: Climate Change in the Arctic
Branwen Williams studies ancient coral and algae from the Arctic Ocean to uncover changes in the ocean that affect our climate.

17:39 Skylar Tibbits: Designing "Smart" Tech
With rapid liquid printing, Skylar Tibbits designs “smart” wood, metal, and plastic!

21:05 Munazza Alam: Hunting for Earth-Like Planets
Nat Geo explorer Munazza Alam is an astronomer on a mission: to find the next Earth. She uses giant telescopes to search for signs of hidden planets!

24:31 Robert Wood: Microrobotics and Honeybee Drones
The robots of the future are tiny, capable, and… bugs? Explorer Robert Wood draws inspiration from nature to create robotic insects.
