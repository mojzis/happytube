# Meet the Herd: Swazi | San Diego Zoo Safari Park

Meet Swazi, the matriarch of the herd at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park’s all-new Denny Sanford Elephant Valley. 🐘

Elephants live in closely connected, matriarchal, and often multigenerational family groups made up of related females and their young, and those relationships are constantly shifting. 

The herd’s matriarch is Swazi, one of oldest and largest elephants in the group, and also one of the kindest. She uses her quiet confidence to lead and support the herd. Just her presence, and a subtle flare of her ears, is enough to keep potential disagreements at bay. When Swazi isn’t keeping busy as the herd's gentle leader, she loves to swim, and will often bond with the younger ones by joining them for a pool party. 

Through alliances and partnerships across continents and disciplines, San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance supports elephant conservation, including innovative discoveries in elephant behavior, communication, comprehensive health, and movement—alongside around-the-clock efforts rescue, rehabilitate, and reintroduce orphaned elephants back into protected native habitats while supporting coexistence between people and these gentle giants. By learning from and caring for elephants like Swazi at the Safari Park, and sharing that knowledge with conservation partners worldwide, we help to strengthen conservation strategies and create a future where elephant families and herds can continue to thrive.
