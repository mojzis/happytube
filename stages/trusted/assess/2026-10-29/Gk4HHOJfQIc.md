# Elephant Loses Teeth and Shows Off Her New Teeth Coming In!

Asian elephants go through about 6 sets of teeth in their lifetime.  What happens when they lose teeth?  Is there an elephant tooth fairy?  Sort of, but we call them elephant keepers!  Learn how they adjust care when an elephant loses a tooth. #AsianElephantAwarenessMonth

Keeper Hannah Mohr explains how they make adjustments to care for the elephants when they lose teeth and wait for the new ones to come in. To help the tooth fall out they provide a lot of browse, leaves and twigs. As they wait for the new teeth to come in, to aid with digestion, keepers provide them with chopped hay, mineral oil and bran, which is essentially a fiber that helps keep things moving through their GI tract so that there's no blockages.
A nutritionist helps alter their diet during this time and vet staff help when it comes to clean outs and providing fluids.
