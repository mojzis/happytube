# How Is Football Helping Save Zambia’s Carnivores? | Kingdom | BBC Earth

Henry Mwape works for the Zambian Carnivore Program (ZCP), which protects large carnivores such as lions and wild dogs. Henry set up a football team named Mimbulu, which is a local Zambian word for wild dogs. Combining his love for conservation and football, at half time ZCP showcase a play where they educate people to protect their livestock from wild carnivores.
