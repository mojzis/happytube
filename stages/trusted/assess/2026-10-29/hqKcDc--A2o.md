# Prospére's Story: How a Silverback Gorilla Saved His Team from a Mamba

Prospère TEBERD has been a Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden Hoffman Coexistence Fellow since 2024. Graduating from the fellowship this year, Prospère’s work as a Mondika Gorilla Project Site Research Assistant for Congo-Apes in the Djéké Triangle of Congo has allowed him to act as a facilitator of human-wildlife coexistence in the field.

This story is just one powerful example of how consistency and long-term dedication within the conservation space can effectively drive coexistence. Who knew that one day, the species Prospère has devoted his life’s work to protecting, would return the favor and protect him? At the root of conservation change are the connections fostered over time between local communities, wildlife, and the organizations who work to propel these missions forward. If Kingo the silverback gorilla could see the value of this consistency, so much so that he was compelled to warn the trackers of the mamba’s presence, can you imagine the power it holds for local communities whose livelihoods often rely on the land and its resources- the resources that Congo-Apes is working to preserve?
