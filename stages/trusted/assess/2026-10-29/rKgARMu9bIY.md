# Meet The Herd: Khosi | San Diego Zoo Safari Park

Meet Khosi, another amazing elephant at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park's all-new Denny Sanford Elephant Valley 🐘

Khosi, is easy to spot because she’s the unicorn—the only elephant with one tusk! As Umngani’s oldest daughter, Khosi was the herd’s first female calf and everyone immediately adored her. The other adults were very protective of her, and all wanted to take turns babysitting her. Even as a teenager, she is still a social butterfly who has a great relationship with every adult in the group. She is confident and smart, and likes to take the herd on adventures. Along with Swazi, Khosi is the first to discover something going on in the habitat. When an underground pipe was leaking, she smelled it, dug it up, and turned the resulting geyser into a hollow filled with muddy fun for everyone! She’s first into the watering hole for an evening splash, and gets the other little ones to join her for a pool party. It’s no surprise she’s also the best babysitter.

Through alliances and partnerships across continents and disciplines, San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance supports elephant conservation, including innovative discoveries in elephant behavior, communication, comprehensive health, and movement—alongside around-the-clock efforts to rescue, rehabilitate, and reintroduce orphaned elephants back into protected native habitats while supporting coexistence between people and these gentle giants. By learning from and caring for elephants like Khosi at the Safari Park, and sharing that knowledge with conservation partners worldwide, we help to strengthen conservation strategies and create a future where elephant families and herds can thrive.
