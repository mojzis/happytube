# A Day in the Life of the Vet Team | San Diego Zoo Safari Park

What does an ordinary day at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park’s Harter Veterinary Medical Center look like? When your patients include hundreds of species—small, large, feathered, hooved, horned, and scaled—there’s really no such thing as an ordinary day.
 
Though no two days are the same, one thing never changes: the expertise, compassion, and teamwork we bring to treat every single patient.
 
Join us for an "ordinary" day at Harter Veterinary Medical Center and meet some of the people behind the extraordinary care.
