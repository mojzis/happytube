# Branch by Branch: Bowyer Farm Grows and Boxes Browse for Cincinnati Zoo's Animals

Summer is one of Bowyer Farm’s busiest seasons.

Each week, Bowyer provides approximately 40 bundles of browse, weighing about 15 pounds each, for animal habitats throughout the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden. Additional loose browse is harvested weekly for the elephants, while approximately 300 thirty-pound boxes will be frozen before winter to provide browse year-round.

Browse fields established over the past six years have matured into a reliable, sustainable food source for many of the Zoo’s animal residents.
