# Bowyer Farm: Restoring Land for Wildlife, Agriculture, and Future Generations

In 1995, a 529-acre farm was willed to the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden with one important condition: the land could only be developed if it furthered the Zoo's conservation mission. Today, Bowyer Farm has expanded to more than 600 acres and serves as a unique crossover of regenerative agriculture, wildlife conservation, native plant propagation, renewable energy, and ecological restoration. 

Across the property, the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden is restoring native habitats, improving soil and water quality, supporting pollinators and wildlife, and demonstrating how agriculture and conservation can work together to fight biodiversity loss.
