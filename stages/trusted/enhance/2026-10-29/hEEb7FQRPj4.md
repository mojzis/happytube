# Another Cincinnati Zoo Sihek (Guam kingfisher) Begins Journey to Palmyra Atoll

One special bird from Cincinnati Zoo just left and has joined the other nine Sihek (Guam kingfisher) chicks coming from AZA-accredited facilities all over the U.S. that are candidates to travel to Palmyra Atoll later this year. The 7-8 young birds that are selected to travel there will join 9 Sihek that were released on this predator-free island in the Pacific in 2024 as an attempt to reestablish this species that is classified as extinct in the wild. Eventually, the hope is to get these birds back to their native home in Guam.
