# The Hidden World Beneath the Everglades | Going Wild

How do you study a wetland that isn't wet? Soil ecologist and social media sensation Jordon King gives Dr. Rae Wynn-Grant a proper initiation into the Florida Everglades, where even the dirt has a story to tell. But with drought transforming the wetlands in one of the regions driest seasons, they're discovering that what's missing can be just as fascinating as what's beneath their feet.

This season of Going Wild is heading out of the studio and into the wilderness. From Bear Dens in Arkansas to deep sea dives for slave shipwrecks in the Florida Keys, we are getting you closer to the people, the animals, the sounds and the stories that make these wild spaces so magical and so worth protecting.
