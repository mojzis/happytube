# Cincinnati Zoo to Livestream Giraffe Birth for the First Time in Zoo History

“Welcoming a new giraffe calf is an exciting occasion and being able to share this experience with our community, and beyond, in real time makes it even more special,” said Cincinnati Zoo director, Dave Jenike. “This livestream will give people a rare opportunity to experience the wonder of a giraffe birth while learning more about the importance of protecting giraffes in the wild.”
