# Significant Otters | A PBS Nature Love Song

Every donation helps public media: https://tinyurl.com/ottersong

Happy International Sea Otter Week! Celebrate with our brand-new track packed densely (like sea otter fur) with information and steps you can take to protect otters all over the world. Tip: Turn on your captions (or scroll down) so you don't miss a lyric! And sign up for our newsletter -- every email helps public media: https://www.pbs.org/wnet/nature/newsletter/

Introducing Animal Tracks: A Soundtrack for a Better World from PBS NATURE

Finally: music you actually want to hear meets content you'll feel good about consuming! PBS Nature teamed up with renowned musician Phredley Brown (American Idol, Bruno Mars, Katy Perry) to create a family-friendly co-listening experience to inspire listeners to build a brighter future. Each song is packed with information about wild animals and wild places, and includes small actions anyone can take to help protect our natural world.

We can't wait to share more songs with you soon. What other wild animals or places would you like to hear about? Let us know in the comments below.

Tiny Actions to Protect Otters:
Pick up litter and dispose of pet waste in the trash. Don't flush it!
Learn about your local watershed.
Share the facts from this video with a friend.
