# Beginning of a Black Bear's Rescue Journey 🐻 | Cub Camp Full Episode Compilation | Nat Geo Kids

Watch this compilation of three Cub Camp full episodes on Nat Geo Kids YouTube!

00:00 - The Littlest Cub
When a two-week-old black bear cub is abandoned, conservation officers take him to Judy and Roger Stearns at the Black Bear Rescue Manitoba. This is the only place on the Canadian prairies that rescues orphaned bears. Judy and Roger name the little cub Winston and give him round-the-clock care. As Winston grows, so does his bond with his new family, but it can’t last forever. Judy and Roger must suffer the heartbreak of severing their emotional attachment to teach this cub how to be an independent bear in the wild.

42:24 - Meet the X-Girls
The time has come for black bear cub Winston to wean off human contact and discover that he is a bear. As the sole bear at Cub Camp, this may be a tall order. However, Winston’s luck changes when Xiba, Xyla and Xola, three abandoned girl cubs, arrive! After an adjustment period, Judy and Roger attempt to introduce Winston to the new cubs. Acclimating cubs isn’t easy, but after a few failed attempts, the new cubs become best friends with Winston. Just as the family has more than doubled, cubs Yasmine and Yara arrive and will also eventually join the growing family.

1:25:16 -  Becoming a Bear
It’s time for Winston, Xiba, Xyla and Xola to graduate to an outdoor enclosure. New campers, Yara and Yasmine, have changed drastically since their fearful and malnourished arrival. Thanks to Judy and Roger, they have begun to adjust to their new surrounds at the cub camp and are finally thriving. Meanwhile, much older Victor is itching to finally leave the cub camp and join the wild. However, he must first pass a vet check for his hurt leg.
