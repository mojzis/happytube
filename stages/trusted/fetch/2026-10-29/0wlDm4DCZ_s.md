# Cincinnati Zoo's Bowyer Farm: A Pit Stop Travel Essential for Migrating Birds

What Can You Do to Support Birds on Their Migration Journeys?

Plant for birds. Incorporating native plants into your garden is a great way to support migratory birds, as they host many insect species that birds rely on for protein and building up their muscles. When Sylvia evaluates the muscle mass of the birds that she bands, those who have travelled from areas with plentiful trees, shrubs, and perennials are packing on the muscles thanks to native plant insect buffets!

Hold your housecats near and dear. The number one killer of birds in the United States and Canada is domestic and feral cats, with figures ranging from 1.4-3.7 billion bird deaths in the United States alone every year. Keeping your domestic cats inside can help to reduce this

 number, while simultaneously preventing Avian influenza for beloved house cats.
 
Decorate your windows! Window collisions are among the top 3 human-related bird fatality causes, with nearly 60% of all collisions being deadly. By breaking up space on large windows with decals, window paint, or devices like BirdSavers, you can prevent collisions and save bird lives! Try it out this fall migration season.
