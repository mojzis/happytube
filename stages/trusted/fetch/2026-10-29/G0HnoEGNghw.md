# Meet the Herd: Ndlula | San Diego Zoo Safari Park

Meet the extraordinary elephants at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park's all-new Denny Sanford Elephant Valley, beginning with Ndlula. 🐘

Elephant Valley offers a dynamic glimpse into the social structure that shapes elephant life. Elephants live in closely connected, matriarchal, and often multigenerational family groups made up of related females and their young, and those relationships are constantly shifting. 

As second-in-command, Ndlula plays an important role maintaining the social balance of the herd. She is quick to set boundaries and expectations, especially with the younger elephants. These interactions are important in strengthening the relationships and shared knowledge that help a pachy herd thrive.

Through alliances and partnerships across continents and disciplines, San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance supports elephant conservation, including innovative discoveries in elephant behavior, communication, comprehensive health, and movement—alongside around-the-clock efforts rescue, rehabilitate, and reintroduce orphaned elephants back into protected native habitats while supporting coexistence between people and these gentle giants. By learning from and caring for elephants like Ndlula at the Safari Park, and sharing that knowledge with conservation partners worldwide, we help to strengthen conservation strategies and create a future where elephant families and herds can continue to thrive.
