# Saving the African Penguin: How Cincinnati Zoo & SANCCOB Are Changing the Tides

African Penguins are predicted to go extinct in just 9 years. We are committed to changing that. The Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden partners with SANCCOB (Southern African Foundation for the Conservation of Coastal Birds) to rescue, rehabilitate and release seabirds, and to advocate for the environment they depend on. 

Thanks to supporters like you who signed a petition, SANCCOB and BirdLife South Africa won a lawsuit against South Africa's environment ministry, securing protections for African Penguin feeding grounds. 

Every visit to the Zoo and every act of support helps this work.
