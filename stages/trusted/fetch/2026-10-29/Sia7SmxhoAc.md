# From Zoo to Soil: Second Release of Endangered American Burying Beetles at Greenacres Foundation

The Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden is back at the Greenacres Foundation for the second release of endangered American Burying Beetles. The first breeding pairs produced hundreds of offspring in the wild, and Greenacres partners later found adult offspring. We recently released surplus beetles from the Cincinnati Zoo's breeding program. The hope is these beetles will overwinter on site and show up in spring pitfall traps, proving they can survive Ohio winters. If it works, this could become a model for other institutions to join the effort.
