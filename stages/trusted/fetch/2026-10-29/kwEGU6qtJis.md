# Meet the Herd: Umngani | San Diego Zoo Safari Park

Meet Umngani, one of the exceptional elephants at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park's all-new Denny Sanford Elephant Valley 🐘    

The foundation of this close-knit herd is the bond between the three oldest females: Swazi, Ndlula, and Umngani. They were rescued and welcomed to the Safari Park around the same time, so they have a respectful and trusting relationship, like sisters. 

As the least dominant of the three adult females, Umngani has to be the most alert, so her ears are usually perked. She’s the herd’s patient, dedicated, and sweet-natured supermom whose daughters, Khosi and Mkhaya, round out her quirky, fun-loving family group. Umngani is very comfortable with her position in the group and doesn’t challenge the others; instead, she uses her resourcefulness and intelligence to cruise the area and pick up browse and other items the other elephants may have left behind. Her calm contentedness has allowed her daughters to become very confident members of the herd, even though their mom has the lowest rank.  

Through alliances and partnerships across continents and disciplines, San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance supports elephant conservation, including innovative discoveries in elephant behavior, communication, comprehensive health, and movement—alongside around-the-clock efforts to rescue, rehabilitate, and reintroduce orphaned elephants back into protected native habitats while supporting coexistence between people and these gentle giants. By learning from and caring for elephants like Umngani at the Safari Park, and sharing that knowledge with conservation partners worldwide, we help to strengthen conservation strategies and create a future where elephant families and herds can thrive.
