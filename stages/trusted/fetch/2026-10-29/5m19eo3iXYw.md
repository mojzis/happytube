# Jack O’Lantern Glow at the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden

Jack O’Lantern Glow, presented by Medpace, is back for its third year at the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden!

October 2-31, 2026. Closed Oct 5, 12 & 19

Jack O’ Lantern Glow is an immersive walkthrough experience with more than 6,000 hand-carved pumpkins and fall displays along a half-mile trail in the Zoo, making it the largest of its kind in the Tri-state area! Experience the grandeur of fall at the Cincinnati Zoo, where it transforms into a mesmerizing wonderland of illuminated jack o’lanterns, breathtaking displays, and special effects that elevate your autumn nights to extraordinary heights. The family-friendly event is great for all ages.

Although the jack o’lanterns are the real stars of this event, Reptile House and Night Hunters will be open nightly until 9pm.
