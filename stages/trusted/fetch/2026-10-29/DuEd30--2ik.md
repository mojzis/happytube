# How to make sushi rice at home

Cooking fish at home sometimes feels intimidating… When it comes to making homemade sushi, the rice can be just as tricky! 

Whether you’re rolling it with fresh cucumber, or building a non-traditional poke bowl masterpiece, properly cooked sushi rice makes all the difference. We tapped sushi chef Hijame Sato, a James Beard Award-winning chef and owner of Sazanami, to teach us how to make the perfect sushi rice at home—no extra tools needed! 

Chef Sato walks us through choosing the right sushi rice vinegar, seasoning your sushi rice, the difference between short-grain and long-grain rice, and how to make sushi rice in both a rice cooker and on your stovetop.
